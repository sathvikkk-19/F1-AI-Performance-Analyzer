Compliance and Data Governance

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 1, 2026 | Revision: 2026.4

1. Information We Collect

The F1 2026 Performance Simulator collects operational data necessary for computational race strategy modeling and user authentication:

  • Account Credentials: Username and salted session credentials used to maintain simulator state and career progress.
  • Telemetry Configuration Inputs: Circuit selections, downforce settings, drag configurations, tire degradation thresholds, and weather profiles input into the strategy engine.
  • Local State and Preferences: Session cookies and local storage tokens stored directly on your client device to preserve active team selection and constructor dashboard state.

2. Processing and Simulation Execution

Simulation parameters are processed locally and through our secure computational backend API. Strategy calculations, tire wear progressions, and aerodynamic delta simulations execute transiently without persistent logging of identifiable telemetry requests.

We do not sell, rent, or commercialize telemetry configurations or individual race simulation queries to third-party advertising networks.

3. Cookies and Client-Side Storage

This platform utilizes strict functional storage mechanisms:

  • Authentication Tokens: Secure HTTP cookies validating session integrity.
  • Session State: In-browser memory caching for instantaneous circuit profile retrieval and career standings.

4. Data Security and Infrastructure

Industry-standard TLS encryption is enforced across all communication channels between your browser and our computational endpoints. Session tokens are hashed and validated with strict CORS boundaries.

5. Contact and Inquiries

For questions regarding telemetry data handling or account retention, contact the engineering desk at operations@f1-performance-engine.internal or open an issue on the project repository.
